Our Story

Answering the Call to End Stigma

IFEA - South Sudan is a national organization dedicated to epilepsy awareness, stigma reduction, and community education. Based in Rock City, Juba, we work across South Sudan with a focus on Mvolo County, Western Equatoria State, where onchocerciasis-associated epilepsy is prevalent but masked by stigma and misinformation.

Mission: To educate, empower, and transform society for inclusive participation of people with epilepsy through awareness, advocacy, and stigma reduction.

Vision: A South Sudan where people with epilepsy live free from stigma and discrimination, with access to care and support.

Areas of Focus

What We Do

We deliver integrated programs that address epilepsy awareness, stigma reduction, and community empowerment.

Epilepsy Awareness

Raising public understanding of epilepsy, its causes, and first aid response to seizures.

  • Community awareness campaigns
  • School education programs
  • Media & radio outreach
Learn more

Stigma Reduction

Combating negative beliefs and discrimination through stigma bench sessions and peer support.

  • Stigma bench activities
  • Caregiver support groups
  • Community dialogue forums
Learn more

Community Education

Empowering communities with accurate information to reduce misinformation and discrimination.

  • Health talks in villages
  • Training of local volunteers
  • Distribution of IEC materials
Learn more

Advocacy

Advocating for the rights and inclusion of people with epilepsy at local, national, and global levels.

  • WHO IGAP implementation
  • Policy engagement
  • Participation in global forums
Learn more

Health & Nutrition

Addressing underlying causes like cerebral malaria and onchocerciasis that can lead to epilepsy.

  • Malaria prevention education
  • Onchocerciasis screening support
  • Nutrition awareness
Learn more

Research

Generating evidence on epilepsy prevalence and stigma to inform better interventions.

  • Community surveys
  • Data collection & analysis
  • Publication of findings
Learn more
